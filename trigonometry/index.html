Big picture
Stage 1 · static shapes. Right triangles with the same angle share the same side ratios, so a shadow reveals a height.
Stage 2 · spinning waves. Fix the hypotenuse at 1 and trig becomes the coordinates (cos θ, sin θ), which unroll into waves.
Stage 3 · inverses, complex numbers, series. arcsin needs a restricted domain, eiθ packs cos and sin into one rotation, and polynomials approximate sin.

Surveying & astronomy

Eratosthenes used a shadow angle to estimate Earth’s size, and Galileo estimated lunar mountain heights from their shadows. Surveyors still rely on the same ratios.

Game physics

A character moving at speed v at angle θ has vx = v·cos θ and vy = v·sin θ.

Roofs & ramps

Tangent is rise ÷ run, which sets the slope of roofs, bridges and wheelchair ramps.

AC electricity

Generators spin magnets in a circle, giving the 50/60 Hz sine wave at your socket.

Sound

A pure tone is a sine wave; real notes are sums of sines. Noise-cancelling headphones add an inverted copy (shifted by π) to cancel much of the noise.

GPS

Satellite positions along their orbits are computed with cos and sin; your phone times signals from 4+ satellites to locate you within metres.

Inverse sine: finding the unique angle

A horizontal line hits sin θ infinitely often (red). arcsin picks the single green point in [−90°, +90°].
Principal output

A function returns one answer per input, so arcsin is defined on the restricted domain [−90°, +90°]. Otherwise arcsin(0.5) could be 30°, 150°, 390° …

Robotics: inverse kinematics turns a target (x, y) into joint angles using atan2 and arccos (law of cosines).

Euler’s formula: eiθ = cos θ + i·sin θ

Multiplying by i turns a point by 90°. An imaginary exponent turns growth into rotation.

Euler’s identity: eiπ + 1 = 0 links e, i, π, 1 and 0.

Signals: Wi-Fi, 5G and MRI rely on the Fast Fourier Transform, built on eiθ, to separate many overlapping frequency components.
